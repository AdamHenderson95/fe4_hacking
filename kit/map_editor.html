How to

  • Click a tile on the right, then paint on the map.
  • Drag across the tile sheet to pick a block (castles, bridges).
  • Right-click the map, or use Copy, to grab tiles from the map itself.
  • Terrain follows the tile automatically: water tiles block walking.
  • Keep the map's size; rebuild with fe4hack.py build.
